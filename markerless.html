Credits

Marine data

Sea surface temperature from Open-Meteo, generated using data from the German Weather Service (DWD). Licensed CC BY 4.0.

The temperature-to-bleaching mapping is a simplified model for visualisation. Real bleaching depends on heat stress accumulated over weeks, not a single reading.

3D models

Coral model by JonathanFreire and reef model by rkuhlf, both via Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0.

Libraries

A-Frame and MindAR, both MIT licensed. <model-viewer> by Google, Apache 2.0, used to hand the reef to ARKit through AR Quick Look on iOS and to Scene Viewer on Android. Fish, particles and every sound in the app are generated procedurally in code.
