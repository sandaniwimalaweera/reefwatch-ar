ReefWatch AR

Coral loses its colour when the sea gets too warm. It is happening off Trincomalee and Hikkaduwa, and almost nobody can see it happen.

This puts a reef on the floor in front of you, and lets today's real sea temperature decide whether it lives.

Image tracking Scan the card Point your camera at the printed reef card. A single coral appears on it, with fish. Tap the coral to watch it bleach. Open Plane detection Place a reef Scan your floor and drop a whole reef section into the room. Live sea temperature sets its colour. Move the slider to change the water. Open
