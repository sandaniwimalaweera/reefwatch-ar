Marine data

Sea surface temperature from Open-Meteo, generated using data from the German Weather Service (DWD). Licensed CC BY 4.0.

The temperature-to-bleaching mapping is a simplified model for visualisation. Real bleaching depends on heat stress accumulated over weeks, not a single reading.
